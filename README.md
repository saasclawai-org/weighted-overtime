# Weighted Overtime Calculator

**Free FLSA weighted overtime calculator — get the blended regular rate right when an employee has multiple pay rates, tips, bonuses, or shift differentials.**

**→ Try it live: https://weighted-overtime-calculator.saasclaw.ai**

## The problem it solves

When a non-exempt employee works overtime at different hourly rates — or earns tips, nondiscretionary bonuses, or shift differentials — the FLSA requires overtime to be paid on the **weighted-average ("blended") regular rate**, not just the rate of the OT shift. Miscalculating it is one of the most common wage-and-hour violations, and the math gets ugly fast with semi-monthly or bi-weekly payroll.

This calculator:

- Blends **multiple pay rates** into the correct FLSA regular rate
- Folds in **tips, bonuses, and shift differentials**
- Supports weekly and bi-weekly payroll periods
- Applies **minimum wage adjustments** (including tipped rates)
- Runs the whole calculation **client-side** — no data ever leaves the browser

## Tech
